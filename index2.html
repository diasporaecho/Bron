MY VERDICT

You can pick Jordan.
Just don’t tell me why I picked Bron.

This ain’t about something LeBron said in an interview. It’s about the scoring, passing, rebounding, defense, IQ, power, speed, versatility, pressure moments and longevity I’ve seen with my own eyes.

I don’t need everybody to agree. But my GOAT case came from the court — not from Bron crowning himself.
